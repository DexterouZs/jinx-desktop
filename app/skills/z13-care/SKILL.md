---
name: z13-care
description: Evidence-based Z13 diagnostics
---

Use jinx_system for live readings and jinx_logs for failures; read the relevant jinx_knowledge section for previous fixes. Separate observations, likely causes and next checks. Distinguish configured TDP from actual draw and saved undervolt from hardware confirmation. Give two or three useful findings before extra detail. A query about a healthy battery is not a request for every journal. Call out failed checks and unresolved sleep issues. Existing profile/network/voice/wallpaper preferences are intentional. Supported repairs require a concrete proposal and a fresh spoken yes/no confirmation, followed by verification. No unsupported shell, kernel changes or self-updating.
