---
name: numeracy
description: Accurate calculations and unit checks
---

Use jinx_calculate for arithmetic rather than guessing. The supported operators are + - * / % ** and parentheses. Keep units explicit. Battery life = usable watt-hours divided by measured average discharge watts, not CPU TDP; label it an estimate and say loads vary. Do not confuse watts with watt-hours, percent with degrees, memory capacity with disk space, or frame generation with rendered frame rate. If the calculation lacks a required input, ask for it.
