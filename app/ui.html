Jinx

Connecting…
Your desktop companion
Click Start conversation once. Jinx detects when you finish speaking, answers, then listens again. Click End conversation to stop. Her microphone closes while she answers. Audio stays local and is not retained.
Voice & system assistant
Speak English or German; replies are always in English.
NPU speech runs locally and unloads after a minute idle.
Free offline speech. No account or monthly quota.

Try “Open Firefox”, “Open my TDP controls”, “Check my battery and temperatures”, or “Check the GPU logs from the previous boot”.

Jinx can launch apps and inspect the system directly. Supported maintenance shows a concrete review before applying. She has a dated guide to this Z13’s setup and known issues.
Messaging

Jinx reads the recipient and message aloud, then waits for yes or send. Changing the message, stopping speech or starting another task requires a new read-back.

WhatsApp is not connected.

Link your phone in the ZapZap app. ZapZap has its own standalone window and login. Chat messages are sent only after your spoken confirmation. Personal Discord sending is not connected; Jinx can keep a draft.

Jinx can look up an exact WhatsApp contact name and verify its phone number. If the name is ambiguous or unavailable, save the exact contact below.

Say “Tell Alex that I’m home soon on WhatsApp”. After the read-back, say “yes” or “send”. Say “change the message to …” for a new read-back.







Software installs
Ask “Install VLC” or enter an app below. Jinx checks configured CachyOS/Arch repositories and shows a package preview. KDE handles administrator authentication.
Repository apps only. AUR or downloaded installers need a separate review. Stop conversation does not interrupt an authorised package transaction.


Screen & pages
“Look at my screen” takes one snapshot of the current monitor. “Read this page” reads its visible text. Images are processed locally and removed after capture; blue screen corners stay visible while Jinx looks or reads. No continuous recording or automatic scrolling.
Use Jinx’s avatar menu or Meta+J while the page you want is visible.


Web & learning
Search terms go to DuckDuckGo; public websites receive page requests. Your model, voice and notebook stay local. No browser logins or home-network access.
Load a page into Reading & writing, or ask “Search the web for…” / “Learn about…”. Learning saves a dated, source-linked note for later recall. It does not retrain the model.


Reading & writing




Open a document or paste text below. Only text you share is used.





For translation or a specific rewrite, share the text then ask in chat—for example, “Translate my document into German”. Reading uses passages of up to6,000 characters; Read next continues. PDF text, DOCX, ODT, TXT and Markdown are supported; scanned images need OCR.







Saved drafts go to Documents/Jinx. Nothing is sent or typed into another app.




What Jinx can help with
Read shared text aloud; summarise documents; draft and improve writing; translate; take dictation; calculate; research websites and recall saved knowledge; open and install repository apps; check Z13 readings and logs; set local timers, alarms and reminders; save dictated notes; propose supported repairs.
Try “Set a timer for five minutes named tea”, “Remind me in ten minutes to check the oven”, “Set an alarm for 7 am”, “What is twenty three times seventeen?”, “Convert five miles to kilometres”, or “Write this down: buy coffee tomorrow”. Timers and reminders notify while Jinx runs, including catch-up after resume; they do not wake a sleeping computer.
These are reviewed local skills and tools. The underlying model is still fallible; source material and live measurements take priority over guesses.


Memory & personality
Personal memories and research notes live on this device’s SSD. New conversations keep saved facts, but start with fresh chat context.
For example: “My favourite drink is tea.” Say “Remember that…” to save another fact. Corrections replace a saved preference. Review, edit or delete facts below. Personality is in Jinx/PERSONALITY.md. Private daily backups stay on the SSD for 14 days.
Messages, active message drafts, phone numbers, approvals and shared-source turns are excluded. Turning this off stops new episodes; existing memories remain.


Reminders & conversation
Saved memories remain. Review, correct or delete them in Memory & personality.
